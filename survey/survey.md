# Towards Understanding WebView Insecurities

Thank you for taking part in this survey and contributing towards enhancing the security of Android WebView.

This survey takes approximately **10 minutes** of your time and consists of **5 short blocks of questions**. You **may skip any question** at any time. At the end of the survey, you will have the possibility to opt-in to receiving a preprint of the resulting paper.

Your answers are **strictly confidential** and resulting insights will only be published in **aggregated form**.
